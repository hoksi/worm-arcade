Worm Arcade: Super Tank

Worm LabGitHub한국어

The real wiring diagram of the worm C. elegans plays the 1981 arcade game Super Tank. The wiring is unchanged; evolution trained only a few values per neuron.

Without the ROM file you see a recording; choose the ROM and it plays live in your browser. In the Worm Lab you can put out food and predators and watch how it moves.
